01 Robotics / Motion control

OMNICAR — Omnidirectional robotic platform

Redesign, implementation and experimental validation of a complete omnidirectional robot, from the mechanical structure and electrical system to embedded firmware, ROS2 and high-level controllers.

My contribution

I redesigned the CAD structure, integrated the electronics, developed modular ESP32 firmware and a PI wheel-speed controller, migrated the architecture to ROS2 Jazzy and compared three motion-control strategies through reproducible experiments.

0.0469 → 0.0330 m lateral RMSE, LIN-05 vs MPC-06
50 / 100 / 200 Hz encoder estimation rates validated
3 high-level controllers compared

  • ESP32
  • C++

Profile

Curious by nature. Rigorous by method.

I am an Automation Engineering student interested in the point where mathematical models, software and physical systems meet. I enjoy understanding the complete system, not only the algorithm inside it.

I work with a direct, structured approach: define the problem, build a measurable test, inspect the limits and improve the result. Modern AI-assisted workflows help me prototype faster, while architecture, validation and final implementation remain under my responsibility.
